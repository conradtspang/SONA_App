# How to Install the SONA App on Your Phone

Follow these simple steps to download and install the SONA App directly from our official release page.

## For Android Users

1. **Visit the Download Page:** Open the web browser on your phone and go to the following link:
   [https://github.com/conradtspang/SONA/releases](https://github.com/conradtspang/SONA/releases)

   ```
   Once I finalize the App, I will release it to Google Play for download.
   ```

2. **Download the App:** 
   - Look for the **Latest** release at the top of the page.
   - Scroll down to the **Assets** section of that release.
   - Tap on the file `main.apk` to start downloading it to your device.

3. **Allow Installation (If Prompted):**
   - Once the download is complete, tap **Open** on the downloaded file notification.
   - *Note:* Your phone might display a security warning asking for permission to install unknown apps. If this happens, tap **Settings** on the prompt and turn on the switch for **"Allow from this source"**.

4. **Install:**
   - Return to the installation screen and tap **Install**.
   - Wait a few moments for the installation process to complete.

5. **Open the App:** Tap **Open** to launch the SONA app and log in!

---

*Note for iOS (iPhone) Users: Direct installation via GitHub is typically for Android devices. If you are on an iPhone, please look out for an official App Store release or a TestFlight invitation link.*

# How to Set Up and Use the SONA App

Welcome to the SONA App! Once installed, follow this guide to set up your profile and navigate the app's features smoothly.

## 1. Getting Started (Login / Sign Up)
When you first open the SONA app, you will be prompted to access your account:
*   **Existing Users:** Enter your **Username** and 4-digit **Passcode**, then tap **Sign in**.
*   **New Users:** Tap **Sign up** to create a new account.

## 2. Personalize Your Experience
After logging in, you'll need to set up your preferences for alerts and app language:
*   **Language Selection:** Tap your preferred language from the available options (e.g., English, Español, Italiano, Deutsch, 日本語, 中文, हिंदी).
*   **Warning Voice Selection:** At the bottom of the screen, tap the icon to select your preferred warning voice.

## 3. The Main Dashboard (App Modes)
Once your preferences are set, you will reach the main dashboard. Here, you can access all five core features of the SONA app:

### 📷 1. Camera Calibration & Mapping (First-Time Setup)
*Use this mode to ensure the app's cameras are accurately calibrated to your physical environment.*
*   Tap **Camera Calibration & Mapping**.
*   **If you are a New User:** The app will guide you through **Camera Calibration** first. Once calibrated, proceed to **Mapping** to map your surroundings.
*   **If you are an Existing User in a New Environment:** You can skip calibration and proceed directly to **Mapping** your new surroundings.

### 🎯 2. Live Object Navigation
*Use this mode for real-time navigation assistance.*
*   Tap **Live Object Navigation** from the main menu.
*   Press **"start detection"** to begin analyzing your surroundings for obstacles.

### 👁️ 3. Live Remote Viewing
*Use this mode to securely connect for remote assistance.*
*   Tap **Live Remote Viewing**.
*   Enter the specific **username and passcode** required for the connection.
*   Press **Connect** to start the live remote session.

### 🎵 4. Privacy Idle Music
*Use this mode to relax with customized audio and visuals when not navigating.*
*   Tap **Privacy Idle Music**.
*   You have two options:
    *   **Upload:** Add new local music or images from your device.
    *   **Play:** Select and play music or view images you have already saved.

### 📚 5. Saved Obstacle Library
*Use this mode to review a history of obstacles the app has detected.*
*   Tap **Saved Obstacle Library**.
*   You can filter and **select the day, time, or label** to find specific saved obstacles you want to review.

---

## Logging Out
When you are finished using the app, tap the **Sign Out & Exit** button at the bottom of the main dashboard to securely log out.
